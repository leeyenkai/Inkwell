
Publishing used to mean a text editor, a terminal and three commits called "published draft again". Now a post starts here.[^1]

## What changed

- **Drafts stay on this Mac** until they are ready.
- Images are resized and *cleaned of location data* on the way in.
- The preview uses the site's own layouts and CSS.
