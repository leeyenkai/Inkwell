The ferry leaves at seven, which is the only reason anyone takes the long way round. By the time the harbour wakes up you are already on the cliff path, with the town folding itself away behind you.

## The first climb

The path rises sharply out of the village and does not apologise for it. At the top there is a bench, a view across the bay, and usually a dog that wants your sandwich.

> Walk until the map runs out, then walk a little further.

From there it is easy going: a long, level stretch above the water, with the islands lined up like stepping stones. I counted eleven boats and one heron, which looked unimpressed by all of us.

The second ferry brings you back in time for lunch, if you do not stop too often. I always stop too often.
