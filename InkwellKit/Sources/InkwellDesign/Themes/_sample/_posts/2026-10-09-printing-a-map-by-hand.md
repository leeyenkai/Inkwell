I wanted a map of the valley that I could hang on the wall, so I cut it in three layers: the river, the contours and the paths. Each layer is its own block and its own colour.

The contours took the longest. Every line is a small decision about what to leave out, and I kept leaving out too little. The final block has about half the lines of the first one, and it is much better for it.

Registration is the hard part. A millimetre off and the paths wander into the river. I made a simple jig from scrap wood, and after a dozen spoiled sheets the layers started to line up.

There are twenty prints. Two of them are good.
