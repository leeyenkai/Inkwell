We left the hut at four, by head torch, and reached the ridge as the sky turned from black to a deep, clear blue. Nobody spoke much. It was too cold, and there was too much to look at.

Above the snow line the world simplifies. There is the ridge, the sky and the next step, and very little else. It is the closest thing I know to a quiet mind.

The descent was slower than the climb, as it always is. Knees complain, the light flattens, and the hut seems to move further away the longer you walk towards it.
