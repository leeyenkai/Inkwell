The best light of the day lasts about twenty minutes, and it never arrives where you expect it. The trick is to be somewhere good a little early, and then to wait.

I set up above the lake with a flask of tea and watched the hills go from green to violet to almost black. The water held the last of the colour long after the sky had let it go.

Three photographs from the evening made it home. This is my favourite.
