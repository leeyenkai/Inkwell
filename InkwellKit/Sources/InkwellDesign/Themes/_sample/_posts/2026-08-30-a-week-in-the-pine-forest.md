The cabin had a stove, a table and a window that looked straight into the trees. For a week, that was enough.

Most mornings I walked the same loop: down to the stream, along the old logging track, and back up through the pines. By the end of the week I knew where the woodpecker would be.

![Hills in the evening](/assets/images/meadow.jpg)

I read two books, wrote very little and slept better than I have in years. I am already planning to go back.
