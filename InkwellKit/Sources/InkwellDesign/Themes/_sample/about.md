I am Sam, a cartographer by training and a walker by habit. This site is where I keep notes from the path: routes worth repeating, things I made along the way, and photographs that came out better than expected.

## What you will find here

- Long walks, written up a week or two later
- Small projects, mostly maps and prints
- A photograph now and then

> The best way to know a place is to arrive on foot.
